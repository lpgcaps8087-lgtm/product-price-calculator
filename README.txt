PRODUCT PRICE CALCULATOR — APP FILES

Included fields:
- Article name, article weight (grams), order quantity
- Material rate per kg and injected material allowance (default 2%)
- Spray rate, cycle time, mould cavity
- Machine, labour and electric cost per hour
- Rent per day, production hours per day
- Packing cost, other expenses, profit margin, GST rate
- Section 05: Your calculated price (kept)

How to use:
1. Upload these files to the root of a website hosted over HTTPS (for example, a GitHub Pages site).
2. Open the website in Chrome on your Android phone.
3. Use Chrome menu > Add to Home screen / Install app.

This ZIP is the app package, not a published website. A hosted HTTPS address is needed for installation as a PWA.
Note: The handwritten second field appears to say “Article wt.”, so it is labelled Article weight (grams). If you meant article quantity instead, change the label or tell me.
